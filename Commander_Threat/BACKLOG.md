# Commander Threat — Backlog

Deliberately not built. Each entry says why it lost the scope argument.

- **Multi-mob sweep for the Damage role.** AoE overnuke detection (your
  worst scaled % across every plate, the healer treatment) is real, but
  Damage's Omen-shaped job is the current target; adding a second number
  source to that headline needs its own design pass.
- **Tank per-mob grid.** Every engaged plate as a row with grip state —
  the LOOSE count expanded into names. The killer tank feature, and a
  whole second board; the footer count ships first.
- **TPS history sparkline.** The engine's EMA is enough for a column;
  history buckets are Meters' territory and Meters owns graphs.
- **Misdirection / Salvation / Tranquil Air awareness.** Threat-modifier
  tags on rows. TBC-real, but aura scanning belongs to
  Afflictions/Shield patterns and the client's numbers already include
  their effects.
- **Per-role WarnAt memory.** One slider serves all three roles today;
  add role-keyed thresholds only if real play shows tanks and DPS wanting
  different lines on the same character.
- **Absolute threat / raw % columns.** %-scaled is the one honest number;
  absolute values are trivia mid-pull.
- **Click a bar to target.** Needs SecureUnitButtonTemplate rows bound out
  of combat with a frozen sort order (the Shield ClickCast tradeoff) —
  the cost (no urgency sorting in that mode) outweighs a convenience the
  target frame already provides.
- **Raid-broadcast anything.** KTM's ghost stays buried; per-client truth
  from the API needs no sync, and the public-output policy keeps chat
  clean.
